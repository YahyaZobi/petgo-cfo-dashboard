PETGO FINANCE CFO DASHBOARD — OVERVIEW AND WALKTHROUGH
========================================================
(Source document for NotebookLM video/podcast generation)


WHAT THIS APP IS
-----------------
The PETGO Finance CFO Dashboard is a single, web-based application built to give
PETGO's leadership — the CFO and CEO in particular — one place to see and manage
the financial and operational health of the company. Instead of switching between
separate Excel sheets for budgets, payroll, invoices, contracts, and project
spending, everything lives in one connected dashboard with a clean, modern
interface designed to be read at a glance and drilled into when needed.

The app currently runs directly in a web browser. It was built quickly and
deliberately as a lightweight, no-server tool so it could be stood up fast and
customised easily for PETGO's specific structure (departments, employees,
currency, branding). It optionally connects to Microsoft 365 (OneDrive /
SharePoint) so that some data can be pulled live from existing company Excel
files rather than re-entered by hand.


WHO USES IT AND WHY IT EXISTS
-------------------------------
The primary users are the CFO and the CEO, with the finance and HR teams doing
day-to-day data entry. The purpose is simple: give the people who make financial
and strategic decisions a single, real-time picture of:

  - How much money is coming in and going out
  - Whether departments are on budget or over budget
  - Which invoices are overdue and which contracts are about to expire
  - What the company is spending on active projects versus what was budgeted
  - Who is on the team, what they're paid, and when their leave or contracts
    are due

The bigger strategic goal — the reason this dashboard matters beyond day-to-day
bookkeeping — is to evolve it into a tool that also tracks PETGO's oil and gas
PROJECTS specifically: their progress, their budgets versus actual spend, and
their position across the upstream (exploration and production) and downstream
(refining, distribution, sales) parts of the business. The Projects module in
the dashboard today is the seed of that: as the company's project data grows,
this is the part of the app that will expand the most.


HOW THE APP IS ORGANISED
--------------------------
The app has a dark, modern sidebar on the left with three groups of sections:
"Menu" (general tools), "Finance" (money-related modules), and "People"
(HR-related modules). Clicking any item switches the main panel on the right —
nothing reloads, it all feels instant, like a native desktop tool rather than a
web page.


SECTION-BY-SECTION WALKTHROUGH
=================================

1. DASHBOARD (Executive Overview)
   The landing screen after login. Shows top-line KPI cards — Total Revenue,
   Total Expenses, Net Profit, Headcount — plus a Revenue vs. Expenses trend
   chart, a recent activity feed, and a company announcements banner. This is
   the "30-second glance" screen: if the CEO only has a minute, this is what
   they look at.

2. ANALYTICS
   A cross-module data visualisation area. It automatically detects the shape
   of the underlying data and picks the most sensible chart type to display it
   — useful for spotting patterns across departments or time periods without
   building a chart manually.

3. DOCUMENTS (My Documents)
   A simple file cabinet inside the app — upload, view, and delete company
   documents directly, with file type icons and size shown.

4. TEAM DIRECTORY
   A living list of every employee: photo, title, department, email, and
   phone. This directory feeds other modules automatically — for example, the
   payroll and contracts sections cross-reference names from here, and the
   org chart is generated from it.

5. AUDIT LOG
   A timestamped history of every create, edit, and delete action taken across
   the whole app. This is the accountability trail — who changed what, and
   when.

6. SETTINGS
   Where the app is configured: language (Arabic/English), currency (LYD, USD,
   EUR), Microsoft data source connections (for pulling live Excel data from
   OneDrive/SharePoint), full data backup and restore, and notification
   preferences.


FINANCE SECTION
-----------------

7. BUDGET TRACKER
   Compares budgeted amounts to actual spend, broken down by department and
   category, with a variance percentage so it's immediately clear who is
   over or under budget. Includes a bar chart comparing budgeted vs. actual
   per department.

8. INVOICES
   The invoice register — vendor, amount, due date, and status (Paid,
   Pending, Overdue, Draft), plus an aging chart showing how overdue invoices
   are piling up.

9. EXPENSE CLAIMS
   Employee-submitted expenses with a built-in approve/reject workflow, so
   the CFO can see what's pending sign-off at any time.

10. PETTY CASH
    A running log of small cash transactions in and out, with a live running
    balance and a breakdown by category.

11. PROJECTS
    Tracks budget versus actual spend per project, with a status flag
    (Active, On Hold, Completed). This is the module that will grow into full
    oil-and-gas project tracking — progress, cost, and eventually
    upstream/downstream categorisation — as PETGO's project portfolio expands.


PEOPLE (HR) SECTION
----------------------

12. LEAVE TRACKER
    Employee leave requests (Annual, Sick, Emergency, Unpaid) with approval
    status, a calendar view, and automatic days-remaining calculations.

13. PAYROLL
    The monthly salary register — base salary, deductions, and net pay per
    employee, with headcount and total payroll KPIs. Includes one-click,
    print-ready PDF payslip generation with PETGO branding, a full earnings
    and deductions breakdown, and the net pay spelled out in words.

14. CONTRACTS
    Tracks every employee contract's expiry date with colour-coded urgency
    (Critical / Warning / Active / Expired), so nothing lapses unnoticed.
    Signed contract PDFs can be attached and viewed directly, along with each
    role's job description.

15. ONBOARDING
    A checklist tracker for getting new employees set up.

16. ORG CHART
    Automatically generated from the Team Directory — CEO at the top,
    departments as branches, employees as leaves. Always up to date because
    it's built from the same data as the directory.

17. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
    Tracks review cycles, ratings, and notes per employee, with a status for
    where each review stands.


FEATURES THAT RUN ACROSS THE WHOLE APP
-----------------------------------------
  - Global Search: search every module at once and jump straight to a result.
  - Smart Notifications: automatic alerts for expiring contracts, overdue
    invoices, and expense claims waiting on approval.
  - Dark/Light Theme: switch the whole app's look with one click.
  - Excel Export: nearly every table can be exported to Excel in one click.
  - Full Backup/Restore: the entire dashboard's data can be exported to a
    single file and restored later.
  - Optional Microsoft 365 Login and Live Data: with an Azure/Microsoft Entra
    ID app registration configured, users can log in with their real
    Microsoft work account, and modules can pull live numbers straight from
    Excel files stored in OneDrive or SharePoint — so the dashboard can
    reflect the same numbers finance already keeps in Excel, without manual
    re-entry.


WHY THIS MATTERS FOR THE CEO
-------------------------------
Today, this dashboard already replaces a scattered set of spreadsheets with one
coherent, branded, easy-to-read view of PETGO's finances and people. The
direction it's heading — and the reason it's worth investing further in — is
becoming the CEO and CFO's single shared window into PETGO's oil and gas
projects specifically: seeing project progress, cost performance, and
upstream/downstream activity in the same place they already check revenue,
payroll, and contracts. The goal is one dashboard, one source of truth, for
both the financial side of the business and the operational project side.
