## Upstream

- **National Oil Corporation (NOC)** — State energy authority overseeing Libya's entire oil and gas sector — upstream licensing, production oversight and export marketing.
- **Arabian Gulf Oil Company (AGOCO)** — Explores and produces crude across eastern Libya — Sarir, Messla and Nafoora — one of the country's largest producers.
- **Waha Oil Company** — Produces crude from the Waha, Gialo and Dahra fields in the Sirte Basin, exporting through the Es Sider terminal.
- **Zueitina Oil Company (ZOC)** — Sirte Basin producer operating the Zueitina export terminal — crude and gas production, processing and lifting.
- **Sirte Oil Company (SOC)** — Produces oil and gas around Marsa el-Brega and runs gas processing plants and pipelines serving the coastal complex.
- **Harouge Oil Operations** — Produces crude from the Amal, En Naga and Ghani area fields, exporting via the Ras Lanuf terminal.
- **Mellitah Oil & Gas B.V.** — Operates western Libya's major oil and gas assets — Wafa, El Feel and offshore Bahr Essalam — feeding the Mellitah export complex.
- **Akakus Oil Operations** — Operates the El Sharara field in the Murzuq Basin — light sweet crude piped to the Zawiya refinery and terminal.
- **Mabrouk Oil Company** — Redeveloping and producing the Mabrouk field cluster in the central Sirte Basin.
- **Sarir Oil Operations Company** — Produces crude and gas from the As-Sarah and Jakhira fields in concessions 96 and 97.
- **Nafusah Oil Operations Company** — Joint venture operator developing Contract Area 47 in the Ghadames Basin, ramping up new light crude production.
- **Murzuq Oil Services Ltd** — Oilfield services for southwest Libya — drilling support, logistics and maintenance for Murzuq Basin operators.
- **Zallaf Libya Company** — NOC's frontier development arm — new fields such as Erawin and planned refinery projects in Libya's southwest.
- **Jowfe Oil Technology** — Manufactures drilling fluids and production chemicals and provides cementing and well-stimulation services.
- **National Drilling & Workover Company (NWD)** — National drilling contractor — rig fleet for development wells, workovers and well maintenance nationwide.
- **North African Geophysical Exploration Co. (NAGECO)** — Acquires and processes 2D/3D seismic and provides geophysical survey services across Libya's basins.
- **Eni North Africa B.V.** — Eni's Libyan operating arm — major offshore and onshore gas producer and partner in the Mellitah complex.
- **Sonatrach** — Algeria's state oil company exploring Libyan acreage in the Ghadames Basin under EPSA terms.
- **National Petroleum Construction Company (NPCC)** — Builds and maintains oil and gas infrastructure — pipelines, tanks and plant — with inspection and testing services.
- **Taknia Libya Engineering** — Engineering services for the oil sector — design, technical studies and EPC support, onshore and offshore.
- **National Catering Company for Oil Fields & Terminals (NOFCAT)** — Catering, accommodation and camp management for oil fields and export terminals across Libya.
- **Petro Air Company** — Air transport for the oil industry — crew rotations and cargo flights between the coast and desert fields.

## Downstream

- **Zawia Oil Refining Company (ARC)** — Refines crude at the 120,000 b/d Zawia refinery — fuels and asphalt for the domestic market.
- **Ras Lanuf Oil & Gas Processing Company (RASCO)** — Runs the Ras Lanuf refining and petrochemical complex — naphtha, ethylene and polyethylene output.
- **Brega Petroleum Marketing Company** — Distributes fuels nationwide — storage depots, aviation fuelling and the country's filling-station network.
- **Libyan Emirates Refining Company (LERCO)** — Joint venture rehabilitating the 220,000 b/d Ras Lanuf refinery toward restart.
- **Libyan Fertilizer Company (LIFECO)** — Produces ammonia and urea fertilizers at the Marsa el-Brega petrochemical complex.
- **OLA Energy** — Pan-African fuel marketer — service stations, lubricants and aviation fuelling across 17 countries.
- **Tamoil** — Markets fuels across Europe through the Tamoil retail network — Oilinvest's downstream arm.
